## About

I recently completed my Swiss Matura at the Freies Gymnasium Zürich, with a specialization in Mathematics and Physics. Before beginning university, I am completing Swiss compulsory military service and taking a gap year.

I am broadly interested in physics, mathematics, computer science, programming, artificial intelligence, simulations, history, and the ways in which technology can be used to explore complicated systems. I have been programming since childhood and mainly work with Python. This blog is intended as a place for me to document, as time allows, some of my projects, experiments, research, and other things I find interesting.

My non-technical interests include reading, skiing, scouting, escape rooms, puzzles, and learning about topics far outside my usual areas of study.
